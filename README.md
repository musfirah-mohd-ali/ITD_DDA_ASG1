# Singapore Heritage Brand (Old Change Kee) Blind Boxes
## Overview
Our AR app allows users to collect virtual Old Chang Kee snacks byt scanning a QR code. Aside from thatthey can place the collectibles in the real world using AR. We used Google Firebase Realtime Database to track the users colectibles and accounts.

## How to use the App
### Key Controls
* __Login/Sign Up:__ Once you open the app you are met with a login and sign up page. Depending on whether you have an account. You can either log in or sing up to make an account that will be tracked via Google Firebase.
* __Scan QR Code:__ Point your camera at the QR Code to spawn a gift box
* __Open Gift Box:__ Tap the "Open Me" buttn on the screen when a gift box appears
* __Collect Collectible:__ Press "Collect Me" button to save the collectible into the database
* __Place Collected Items:__ Click on the "See your Collectibles!" button to go to the page with the camera to place collectibles. Use the panel at the bottom to see which collectible you can place onto the real world environment. 

### Cheats/Hacks/Tips
* You can only spawn collectibles that have been collected from scanned QR code.
* Ensure camera permissions is given when using the app.
* Be in a well lit room and textured area as to not have prefabs move around.

## Platform/Hardware
* __AR Supported Devices:__ Android Phone
* __Google Firebase:__ For realtime tracking of collectibles and user data.

## Limitation/Bugs
* Prefabs will shift, float and move around if the surface you are scanning on is not flat or easily detectable.
* Some phones will have the collectible move around and others don't.
